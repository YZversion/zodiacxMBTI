Use the Libra master only as a strict style and layout reference: bone-ivory and rose-gold Art Nouveau frame, burgundy, dusty-rose and smoke-gray cathedral stained glass, a perfectly balanced circular halo, suspended scales, twin feather arcs, polished stone, floral mosaic, crisp ink, flat cel-shaded anime rendering, aged paper and subtle crackle texture. Keep the complete main subject and every ornate border visible. Fill the whole card with artwork. Use balanced beams, paired arches, counterweights, mirrored geometry, feathers, diplomatic ornaments and elegant symmetry; never use other zodiac emblems.

> No words, letters, numbers, initials, MBTI labels, zodiac names, signatures, watermarks, logos, gibberish, pseudo-writing, title strip, bottom whitespace, caption, plaque, cropped limbs, duplicate body parts, malformed anatomy, extra people, photorealism, 3D rendering, watercolor or chibi styling.
