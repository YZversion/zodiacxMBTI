## Shared prompt framework

> Create one original vertical 3:5 full-bleed Gemini × MBTI tarot card. Use the Gemini master only as a strict reference for its bone-ivory and antique-silver Art Nouveau frame, cobalt and cyan cathedral stained glass, mirrored double-circle halo, crisp ink, flat cel-shaded anime rendering, and aged mosaic/crackle texture. Keep the complete main subject and every ornate border visible. Fill the whole card with artwork. Use paired mirror, twin-profile, air-current, mask, quill, and Gemini duality motifs; never use Aries ram or Taurus bull symbols.

> No words, letters, numbers, initials, MBTI labels, zodiac names, signatures, watermarks, logos, gibberish, pseudo-writing, title strip, bottom whitespace, caption, plaque, cropped limbs, duplicate body parts, malformed anatomy, extra people, photorealism, 3D rendering, watercolor, or chibi styling.
