## 统一提示词框架

> Create one original vertical 3:5 full-bleed Pisces × MBTI tarot card. Use the Pisces master only as a strict style and layout reference: bone-ivory and pale-antique-gold Art Nouveau frame, iridescent teal and lavender cathedral stained glass, a monumental pair of translucent celestial fish circling in a twin-fish halo, moonlit deep water, pearl chains, slow bubbles, dream mist and pale underwater flowers, crisp dark ink, flat cel-shaded anime rendering, aged paper, mosaic and subtle crackle texture. Keep the complete main subject and every ornate border visible. Fill the whole card with artwork. Use paired fish, tides, bubbles, pearls, moon-water, dreams, music, mist and compassionate light; never use other zodiac emblems.

> No words, letters, numbers, initials, MBTI labels, zodiac names, signatures, watermarks, logos, gibberish, pseudo-writing, title strip, bottom whitespace, caption, plaque, cropped limbs, duplicate body parts, malformed anatomy, extra people, photorealism, 3D rendering, watercolor or chibi styling.
