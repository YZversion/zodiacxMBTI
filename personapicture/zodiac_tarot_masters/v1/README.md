# Zodiac tarot masters v1

Generated with the built-in `imagegen` workflow on 2026-07-24.

## Output specification

- 12 complete rectangular zodiac character cards
- Approximate 3:5 aspect ratio (`971/972 × 1619/1620`)
- Six male and six female adult characters
- Full illustration from top to bottom; no large typography-safe blank area
- No generated titles, letters, numbers, signatures, logos, or watermarks
- Shared bone-ivory Art Nouveau frame, stained-glass halo, crisp ink outlines,
  flat cel shading, limited palettes, and printed-paper grain
- The approved Aries image was used as the composition and rendering anchor for
  the other 11 cards
- `example2` images were used only as medium/layout references; no sample
  character, costume, title, or exact composition was copied

## Shared production prompt

Each card used the corresponding character variation from the supplied prompt
set, combined with the following production constraints:

```text
Create one original full rectangular zodiac character tarot-card illustration,
long vertical 3:5.

Match the approved series anchor: intact rounded bone-ivory Art Nouveau outer
frame, pointed ornamental arch, large circular stained-glass zodiac halo,
crisp confident ink outlines, flat cel shading, limited palette, subtle
printed-paper grain, and a premium collectible fantasy-card finish.

One original adult character only, full or near-full body, occupying about 70%
of the card. Express the zodiac through a distinct face, hairstyle, costume,
pose, central halo and one dominant symbolic action.

Fill the complete card interior with finished illustration and balanced
ornament from top to bottom. No large blank region, blank title ribbon,
cartouche, text panel, or typography-safe area.

No readable text, letters, words, numbers, Roman numerals, fake calligraphy,
logo, signature, watermark, real-world religious symbols, extra people,
duplicate body, extra limbs, child, chibi, photorealism, 3D rendering,
oil-painting effect, or deformed outer frame.
```

`contact_sheet.png` is a QA overview and is not a production card.
